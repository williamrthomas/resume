## How I'd operate as a founding FDE

- **Show up to land the customer's actual problem, not the version that fits Anthropic's road map.** The customer will frame their problem in the language of their last vendor. The work is to reframe it in the language of agentic AI without losing the underlying need.
- **Codify patterns as I go.** Every customer engagement should leave behind 1–2 reusable patterns. Not maybe — built into the deliverable. This is how the FDE motion compounds.
- **Borrow the federated model from athena.** AI adoption inside an enterprise doesn't scale by central evangelism; it scales when a federated structure of local champions has air cover and shared infra. This is what I spent 2025 building at athena.
- **Boring infrastructure for customer code.** Customers should be able to run the artifacts I leave behind without me. systemd, modular packages, structured-data-first APIs. Resist the temptation to be clever.
- **Pair customer engagement with public artifacts.** robotics.press exists in part so I have a public production system in the same shape as the customer-facing work. The credibility loop matters in enterprise rooms.

## On judgment

- **Sustained shipping over heroic shipping.** 4,435 GitHub contributions in the last year is the metric, but the relevant signal is consistency — month over month, across day job + side projects + writing + family. The same shape an Anthropic FDE needs to sustain.
- **I don't take low-information big swings.** The brief 2016 Florida move I tried with my family didn't work; we returned to Maine and I joined athena. Took the lesson, moved on, didn't retry the same play later.
- **I notice when someone's not a fit and I say so.** A federated model doesn't survive if leaders won't name the misses.
