# Founder track

Maps to JD requirement `r.former-founder` (explicitly encouraged), `r.high-agency`, `r.tenure`.

The job description ends with: *"Former technical founders are also encouraged to apply."* I am one. I've shipped four ventures and a fifth side family business, plus parallel consulting practices across two decades.

## Q5 Labs — sports nutrition brand (2012 – 2016)

Bootstrapped a niche sports-nutrition brand for combat athletes. Naturally formulated supplements designed to speed recovery and enhance training capacity.

- **Built the full product line** and brand concept.
- **Designed the packaging and website** myself (Adobe Photoshop / Illustrator / Premiere).
- **Marketing was 100% content + influencer.** No marketing budget. Athlete partnerships, niche content placement, magazine articles.
- **Wrote for industry print magazines** including four pieces in *Jiu Jitsu Magazine* in 2013.
- **Sales:** $0 → ~$17,847/month in 13 months; peaked at $20K+/month.
- **Exited in 2016** — sold the brand to my BJJ Weekly partner (who had started Origin).

What this proves for FDE: I know what it's like to ship a product that has to actually work, sell, and survive without a big company underneath it. The discipline transfers.

## BJJ Weekly — niche media venture (May 2010 – Jan 2012)

Co-founded with my partner Pete. Weekly online news + education site for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu enthusiasts.

- **Newsletter:** grew 0 → **19,764 double opt-in subscribers** at a **74% open rate**.
- **YouTube:** **7M+ video views** in 18 months.
- **Largest blog in the niche** within 18 months.
- Traveled with film crew to shoot instructional videos with niche-celebrity athletes; wrote weekly editorial.

What this proves for FDE: I've built community, content, and customer trust from zero, in public, with metrics you can verify. That same instinct serves the white-glove deployment FDE motion.

## Thomas Consulting / TCLLC — independent IT consulting (2009 – 2016)

Independent IT consulting practice. Managed services and integrations for dental practices and legal practices in midcoast Maine.

- Hardware/software planning, trouble-ticket resolution, RMM installs, backup and disaster recovery, hardware integration for sensor systems and patient-management software.
- **Customers across two regulated verticals** (healthcare-adjacent dental, legal).

What this proves: I've been the technical person in the room for SMB customers, in regulated industries, for the better part of a decade. The trust-building and project-shaping muscles are well exercised.

## Edit Systems — independent consulting (2003 – 2005)

Independent technology consultant for school systems. Most relevant engagement:

- **Deployed it to every CTE school** in the state.

What this proves: I have done multi-stakeholder, multi-site enterprise deployments before — including in the public sector. The FDE motion is familiar.
