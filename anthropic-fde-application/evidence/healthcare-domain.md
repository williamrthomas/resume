## Products I have operated against

- **Population Health.** Joined in 2017 as athena was integrating Pop Health into athenaOne. The data operation was complex and there was no support structure. I built the entire case-support process, knowledge bases, and operational infrastructure from greenfield. This is the canonical "drop into a customer environment with no scaffolding and build the support model" task — the FDE motion exactly.
- **Health Plan Data Exchange (HPDE).** Platform support across the bidirectional data exchange between athena and health-plan partners.
- **Moment of Care integrations.** Real-time clinical context delivered into the encounter.
- **Enterprise data product (warehouse-backed client offering).** A raw production data store, productized for external clients. It had come up through analytics and IT with no operational structure when I took it on — I built support, ops, and integration to turn it into a real client product. Most recently I've also held interim Product Owner responsibilities during a leadership gap, and led continuity work including release recovery, MFA rollout, and a global Tier 1 support transition.
- **ACO support.** Value-based care, care management, quality reporting, financial analytics. These are exactly the buyer contexts an FDE would meet when an ACO or risk-bearing customer wants to put Claude into their workflow.

## Why this is FDE-shaped

The Platform Operations zone I helped build is a 22-person team that supports approximately **$160M in business** end to end — implementation, integration, terminations, knowledge management, case ops, and cross-functional partnership with Product and Engineering. That's a post-sales platform-deployment motion at enterprise scale: customers' problems land in our zone, and we own them across the lifecycle.

The FDE description — "embedded with strategic customers, builds production AI applications that solve real business problems, codifies repeatable deployment patterns" — is what we already do. With Claude, Snowflake, our federated AI operating model, governed analytics platform, and agent-assisted dev workflows, AI-shaped products are now what we ship.

## `healthcare-data-training` — the curriculum repo

A private repo on my GitHub builds out the thesis that's most directly Anthropic-aligned:

> A new class of healthcare worker is emerging: clinically or operationally fluent, but not technically trained, suddenly empowered by agentic AI to do work that previously required a data engineer. They've spent years consuming pre-built dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, Sigma, Epic Reporting Workbench, athenahealth analytics) — now they want to build. They lack the foundational data fluency to do it well.

The repo names Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor explicitly as the tooling layer. It contains:

- **Research:** 7 personas, 43-course landscape teardown, 10 funnel teardowns.
- **Analysis:** opportunity brief, gap map, formats & artifacts.
- **Curriculum:** 6-module spec, capstone, graduation package, facilitator guide (1,402 lines).
- **GTM:** content calendar, lead-magnet spec + 30 prompts, funnel and feedback loop (~3,000 lines).

This is what the future of FDE-aligned customer enablement looks like in healthcare. I'm already mapping it.
