## Why this repo exists

The job description asks for someone who builds production AI applications, ships MCP servers and sub-agents, codifies repeatable deployment patterns, and operates autonomously in ambiguity. The most direct way to evaluate that is to put the evidence into a structure your interviewers (and Claude, if they use it) can actually navigate.

So instead of a 4-page resume, this repo is the application: a traditional 1-pager up front, a machine-readable ontology in `ontology.json` mapping each JD requirement to specific evidence, and evidence files that any interviewer can drill into based on what they care about.

This format is itself the demonstration. Anthropic FDEs work in customer environments under ambiguity and need to produce artifacts other people can reason over. This repo is one of those artifacts.
